Fokus Hydration is a beverage brand co-founded by popular Indian YouTubers Fukra Insaan (Abhishek Malhan) and Triggered Insaan (Nischay Malhan). Known for their engaging content, both influencers have captivated millions of fans on social media, and now they are set to revolutionize the way people stay hydrated with a line of delicious hydration drinks. Designed to offer a refreshing and health-conscious hydration solution, Fokus Hydration combines natural ingredients with essential nutrients to cater to the modern consumer’s needs.

What is Fokus? The Vision Behind the Brand

Fokus is more than just a beverage brand; it is built on the idea of providing clean and functional hydration. Fukra Insaan and Triggered Insaan collaborated with digital entrepreneurs Mayank Mishra and Ankit Madaan to bring this vision to life. The goal of Fokus is to offer drinks that not only quench thirst but also promote a healthier lifestyle. Fokus (Let’s Focus) was officially launched in February 2025, marking a significant entry into the functional beverage industry.

Fokus Kiwi Lemon (Best Selling Product) – Product Overview

One of the flagship products, Fokus Hydration – Kiwi Lemon, is a unique blend that stands out in the beverage market. This drink is crafted to provide not only a delightful taste but also functional benefits. During its launch, Fokus Kiwi Lemon quickly gained popularity and even went out of stock due to high demand. It has since become the best-selling drink among the available options.

Key Ingredients of Fokus Kiwi Lemon
  • Coconut Water (22%) – Aids in effective hydration with natural electrolytes.
  • Vitamin D3 - Coconut Water (22%)
  • Taurine – Enhances energy metabolism and athletic performance.
  • Ginseng Extract – Improves energy levels and cognitive function.
  • Ginkgo Biloba Extract – Supports mental clarity and focus.
  • Creatine– Helps increase muscle mass and improve strength.
  • Glutamine– Supports muscle recovery and immune function.
  • L-Carnitine L-Tartrate (LCLT)– Plays a role in energy production by transporting fatty acids into the mitochondria.
  • 5-HTP– A serotonin precursor that may help improve mood and reduce anxiety.
  • Inositol– A carbohydrate that influences insulin action and may have mental health benefits.
  • Sucralose– A zero-calorie sweetener providing sweetness without added calories.

These ingredients are meticulously selected to offer a balance of hydration, energy, and mental clarity. The inclusion of natural extracts and essential nutrients makes Fokus Hydration a functional beverage suitable for various occasions.

Fokus Mango Pineapple- Product Overview

Fokus Mango Pineapple is a zero-sugar tropical hydration and energy drink created by Indian YouTubers Fukra Insaan and Triggered Insaan. It blends natural ingredients like coconut water, ginseng, and taurine to offer an affordable alternative to imported sports drinks.

Flavor Profile and Ingredients
  • Taste:A sweet, tropical, and fruity mix of ripe mango and pineapple.
  • Sugar: Contains zero added sugar.
  • Key Components Infused with coconut water, ginseng extract, and taurine for daily hydration and clean energy.
